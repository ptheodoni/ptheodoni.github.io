Women and men in the atlas

For comparison: the 1967 Encyclopedia of Philosophy had articles on over 900 philosophers but no entry for Wollstonecraft, Arendt or Beauvoir; the 2015 Norton Introduction to Philosophy, over 1,000 pages, names no woman philosopher before the mid-20th century (sources). The share here is not the true historical share: it is what a deliberate search finds, and it will change as the atlas grows.

References

For every link: where the later thinker answers the earlier one, and a secondary source that documents it, open access where possible. Links without a reference stay marked as such until one is added.

Be a contributor

The atlas is built by its community. Every contribution goes to the curator, who checks the sources; accepted contributions are credited by name in the Contributors list at the bottom of the page, with what each person added.
