To Err Is Human?

To err is human, but do only humans err like humans? Play against a simulated AI (Meta's model coming soon) on the very same pictures: you may beat it or lose to it; what counts is whether your mistakes are human ones.

Which side has more dots? Level 1 is 10 rounds. After each level you find out who won, then decide whether to go on: 10, 20, 60, 120 rounds, each level harder.
